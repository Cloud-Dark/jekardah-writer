# Headline Angle Types

Six angle types. Each entry: what it does, a calibrated Indonesian example, and when NOT to use it.

## 1. Benefit

States the concrete outcome the reader gets.

> "Cara Nulis Caption yang Gak Kedengeran Kayak Bot — 5 Menit, Gak Perlu Jago Nulis"

**Don't use when:** the body doesn't actually deliver a clear, specific outcome — a vague benefit headline reads as generic marketing copy.

## 2. Curiosity gap

Withholds one specific piece of information the body resolves.

> "Kenapa Draft yang 'Udah Benar' Tetep Kedengeran Kayak AI (Bukan soal Diksi)"

**Don't use when:** the gap can't be closed by a specific body payoff, or when the honest answer is mundane — a curiosity gap that resolves into "it depends" breaks trust.

## 3. Number / list

Signals scope and skimmability with a count.

> "5 Pola Kalimat yang Bikin Tulisan Lo Kedengeran Generic (Cek Draft Lo)"

**Don't use when:** the body's actual structure isn't a clean list — forcing a number onto unstructured content misrepresents the format.

## 4. Contrarian

Challenges a common assumption the body actually disproves or complicates.

> "'Makin Panjang Makin Meyakinkan' Itu Salah — Ini yang Recruiter Beneran Baca"

**Don't use when:** the body doesn't contain an actual contrarian finding — a fake contrarian headline over a conventional body is a broken promise.

## 5. How-to

Promises a procedure or method.

> "Cara Review Draft Sendiri Sebelum Publish, Tanpa Nunggu Orang Lain Baca"

**Don't use when:** the body is a narrative/analysis piece without an actual reproducible procedure.

## 6. Urgency-if-true

Signals timeliness or a closing window — only when the source material actually supports urgency.

> "Sebelum Publish Draft Itu, Cek 3 Hal Ini Dulu"

**Don't use when:** there is no genuine time-sensitivity in the source; manufactured urgency ("jangan sampai ketinggalan!") without a real deadline or consequence is a claim inflation, not a stylistic choice — treat it as a truth-contract violation, not just weak copy.
