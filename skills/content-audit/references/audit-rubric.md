# Audit Scoring Rubric

Each dimension scores 1-5. Anchor every score to specific evidence in the
draft, not a general impression.

## Hook strength

- **5:** First line creates a specific, resolvable question or tension the
  body clearly pays off.
- **3:** Hook is clear but generic — could open several different pieces on
  the same topic.
- **1:** Opens with throat-clearing, a definition, or a claim with no
  payoff in sight (e.g. "Di era digital yang semakin berkembang...").

## Structure/story clarity

- **5:** Clear sequence, a real turn or transformation, information
  released in an order that builds.
- **3:** Readable but flat — points listed without escalation or payoff.
- **1:** Disorganized, repeats itself, or buries the actual point.

## Voice/register fit

- **5:** Diction, rhythm, and code-mix match the stated target register
  consistently across the whole piece.
- **3:** Mostly consistent with a few tonal slips.
- **1:** Register is inconsistent or mismatched to the stated audience.

## Anti-slop (lower slop density scores higher)

- **5:** Concrete nouns/numbers, no filler transitions, no AI-tell phrasing
  ("dalam dunia yang terus berkembang", "di era digital ini").
- **3:** Mostly concrete with a few generic filler sentences.
- **1:** Dominated by generic phrasing interchangeable with any other draft
  on the topic.

## Factual risk (lower risk scores higher)

- **5:** Every claim is attributed or scoped to what the source actually
  supports; no invented numbers or overreaching certainty.
- **3:** Mostly supported, one or two claims slightly overstate certainty.
- **1:** Contains unattributed numbers, invented specifics, or claims
  stronger than the source evidence.

## CTA/payoff clarity

- **5:** The reader knows exactly what to do or take away next; matches
  the stated CTA intent.
- **3:** A CTA exists but is generic or could apply to any piece.
- **1:** No clear CTA, or the ending doesn't resolve what the hook opened.

## Headline accuracy (only when a headline/subject line is present)

- **5:** Headline claim is fully supported by the body, concrete word
  appears early.
- **3:** Accurate but vague or interchangeable with other headlines.
- **1:** Headline promises something the body doesn't deliver.

## Platform fit (only when a target platform is named)

- **5:** Length, segmentation, and layout match the named platform's
  conventions (see `platform-format` skill).
- **3:** Roughly fits but ignores one convention (e.g. no line breaks for
  LinkedIn, caption too long for the IG preview window).
- **1:** Format ignores the platform entirely (e.g. a single wall-of-text
  block submitted as a TikTok script).
